      Common questions

      How does the availability check work?

      We find your address's location with the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup, then compare it with the fiber coverage each provider reports to the FCC (National Broadband Map, ). That gives a preliminary answer in seconds. A person on our team then confirms your exact address with the providers.

      Is it free?

      Yes. Checking your address costs nothing, there's no credit check to see your options, and there's no obligation to sign up.

      Is MyFiberCheck an internet provider?

      No. MyFiberCheck is run by Quota, an independent sales company that's authorized to sell fiber internet from several providers. Plans, prices and installation are provided by the internet provider you choose. We may be paid by the provider when you sign up.

      What happens after I submit my address and number?

      A person on our team checks your exact address with the participating providers and texts you what's actually available. If you want one of the options, we help you set up the order. If you don't, that's it.

      How is my information handled?

      We use your address and number only to check availability and contact you about internet service. We don't sell your information. We share it with a provider only if you ask us to set up service. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

      What if nothing shows up at my address?

      New fiber often isn't on the FCC map yet. Leave your number and we'll check by hand, and we can text you if fiber reaches your street later.
