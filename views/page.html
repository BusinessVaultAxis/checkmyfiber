      FAQ

      Common questions

      Still unsure? Call or text (917) 720-6053.

      How does the availability check work?

      We locate your address with the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder and compare it with fiber coverage providers report to the FCC National Broadband Map (). That's your preliminary result. Our team confirms your exact address with the providers.

      Is MyFiberCheck an internet provider?

      No. MyFiberCheck is operated by Quota, an independent fiber sales company. Plans, prices and installation come from the provider you choose, and we may be paid when you sign up through us.

      Is the service free?

      Yes. Checking is free, there's no credit check to see your options, and there's no obligation to sign up.

      What happens after I submit my address?

      You see a preliminary result right away. If you leave your name and number, our team confirms availability with the providers and texts you your options. You decide from there.

      How is my information used?

      Only to check availability and contact you about internet service. We don't sell it, and we share it with a provider only if you choose to sign up. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. See our Privacy Policy.
