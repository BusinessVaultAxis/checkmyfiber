      Questions, answered

      How does the check work?

      We locate your address with the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup and compare it with fiber coverage that providers report to the FCC. That's your preliminary result. A person on our team confirms the exact address before anything else happens.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. Checking costs nothing, there's no credit check to see your options, and no obligation to sign up.

      Are you an internet provider?

      No. MyFiberCheck is run by Quota, an independent sales company. Plans, prices and installation come from the provider you choose. We may be paid by that provider.

      What happens after I give my number?

      Someone on our team checks your exact address with the providers and texts you what's available. If you want one, we help set up the order. If not, that's the end of it.

      How is my information handled?

      Used only to check availability and contact you about it. Never sold. Shared with a provider only if you ask us to set up service. See our Privacy Policy.

      What if nothing shows up?

      New fiber often isn't on the FCC map yet. Leave your number and we'll check by hand.
