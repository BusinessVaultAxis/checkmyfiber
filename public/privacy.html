Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

MyFiberCheck ("we," "us") is operated by Quota, an independent sales company that sells fiber internet service on behalf of internet providers. We are not an internet service provider. This policy explains what we collect when you use myfibercheck.com and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your address, when you check fiber availability.
  • Your first name and mobile number, and your email if you give it, when you ask us to contact you.
  • How you found us, such as the link or page you came from.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider logs automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To check which fiber providers report service at your address.
  • To call or text you about internet options at that address, if you agreed to be contacted.
  • To place an order with a provider on your behalf, only if you ask us to.
  • To understand which pages and links bring people to the site, so we can improve it.

Text messages and calls

If you check the consent box, you agree that MyFiberCheck (operated by Quota) may call or text the number you provide about internet service, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of buying anything. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With an internet provider, when you ask us to set up service, so they can process your order.
  • With service providers that run the site for us: our web host, our lead-tracking spreadsheet (Google), the U.S. Census Bureau address lookup (which receives the address you type to find its location), and OpenStreetMap's address suggestion service (which receives what you type in the address box).
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Availability data

Coverage results come from the FCC National Broadband Map, which is based on what providers report. Results show where a provider reports service, not a guarantee of service at your home.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact details and address as long as needed to help you with service and keep business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
  • Email us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you. Depending on your state, you may have additional rights under state privacy law, and we will honor them.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
