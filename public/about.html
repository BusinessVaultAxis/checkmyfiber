About MyFiberCheck

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MyFiberCheck is a free tool that shows which fiber internet providers report service at a U.S. street address. It's run by Quota, an independent sales team that sells fiber internet door to door and online. We are not an internet service provider.

How the check works

  1. You enter a street address. We find its location using the U.S. Census Bureau's public address lookup.
  2. We compare that location against fiber coverage that providers report to the Federal Communications Commission (FCC National Broadband Map, June 2026 data).
  3. We show which fiber providers report service at that spot or right next to it, and their fastest advertised speed.
  4. If you ask us to, a real person on our team confirms your exact address with the providers and texts you your options. If you choose a plan, we can set up the order for you.

Which providers we check

Kinetic, Brightspeed, Verizon Fios, AT&T Fiber, Frontier, Quantum Fiber, Fidium, Bluepeak, Ting, Ripple, Wire3, Point Broadband and Clearwave. These are the fiber networks we're authorized to sell. Provider names are trademarks of their owners.

How accurate is it?

The FCC map is the most complete public source of where internet service exists, but it's based on what providers report and is updated about twice a year, so very new fiber construction may not appear yet. That's why we treat every instant result as "likely" and confirm the exact address before anyone signs up for anything. If nothing shows up for your address, we check it by hand.

How we get paid

The check is free. When you choose to sign up for fiber through us, the provider pays us a commission. Plans and prices are set by the provider.

Fiber internet by state and city

We publish fiber coverage summaries for every state and for thousands of U.S. cities and towns, built from the same FCC data. Start from the home page and pick your state at the bottom, or check your address directly.
